Pain Drawing Instructions

Please read carefully:

This tool is provided by Physical Therapy Doctors in Berthoud, CO. When you click Download Drawing, a completed pain drawing form will be saved to your device as a PDF so you can send it back to the front desk. Data entered here is stored only on your device and never transmitted or stored by a server.

Your drawing will be erased when the page is refreshed or closed and after you click the Download Drawing button.

Mark the areas on your body where you feel your pain is. Include all affected areas. Mark areas of radiation. If your pain radiates, draw an arrow from where it starts to where it stops. Please extend the arrow as far as the pain travels.

If you're filling this out on a computer or tablet, use your mouse, trackpad, or finger/stylus to draw directly over the body diagram.

Use the appropriate symbol(s) listed below:

  • Ache: >>>>>
  • Numbness: =====
  • Pins & Needles: 0000
  • Burning: XXXX
  • Stabbing: /////
  • Throbbing: ~~~~~
